# Stakeholder Memo

**Stakeholder:** Portfolio Manager

## Problem

Momentum strategies can perform very differently depending on the market environment. I want to see whether historical market and factor data can help identify periods when momentum is more likely to perform poorly.

## Why It Matters

If a portfolio manager knows momentum is entering a higher-risk period, they may want to review or reduce their exposure.

## What I Will Analyze

I will look at market returns, volatility, and factor data and compare them with future momentum returns.

## Useful Result

The goal is to find a simple way to identify higher-risk periods for momentum.

I would consider the project successful if the patterns I find also appear in different historical periods.
